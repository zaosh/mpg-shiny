November 05, 2026

The Problem

  • Buying a car? Fuel economy matters, but spec sheets are confusing.
  • Question: given weight, horsepower and transmission, how many mpg should I expect?
  • Our app answers instantly, with an uncertainty range.

The Model

Linear regression on the built-in mtcars data (32 cars):

fit <- lm(mpg ~ wt + hp + am, data = mtcars)
round(coef(summary(fit)), 3)
##             Estimate Std. Error t value Pr(>|t|)
## (Intercept)   34.003      2.643  12.867    0.000
## wt            -2.879      0.905  -3.181    0.004
## hp            -0.037      0.010  -3.902    0.001
## am             2.084      1.376   1.514    0.141

R-squared: 0.84

Example Prediction

A 3,000 lb, 120 hp automatic car:

predict(fit, data.frame(wt = 3, hp = 120, am = 0), interval = "prediction")
##       fit     lwr      upr
## 1 20.8697 15.4368 26.30261

Try It

  • Sliders for weight and horsepower, a radio button for transmission
  • Prediction, 95% interval and rating update instantly
